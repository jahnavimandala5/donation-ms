How it works

Three steps to zero waste

From surplus to served — a simple loop that keeps good food out of landfills.

01📤

Donate Surplus

Restaurants, hotels and households list extra, perfectly good food in under two minutes.

02🤝

NGOs Reserve

NGOs, orphanages and shelters near the pickup point reserve the food instantly.

03🍱

Serve the Community

Food is picked up safely and reaches the people who need it most — hunger solved.

Why FoodLink

Built to make giving effortless

Premium tools that turn everyday surplus into community impact.

Live freshness countdowns
